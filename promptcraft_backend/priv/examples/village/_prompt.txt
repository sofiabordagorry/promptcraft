• **Style**: Rustic, practical, medieval village aesthetic
• **Scale**: Small to medium buildings (5x7 to 9x9), village-appropriate
• **Community**: Should feel like part of a functional village

VILLAGE STRUCTURE TYPES:
• **Houses**: 2-3 stories, pitched roofs, multiple windows, chimney optional
• **Farms**: Simple structures with fences, crop plots, hay bales
• **Wells**: Circular or square stone structures with water access
• **Workshops**: Specific purpose buildings (blacksmith, fletcher, etc.)
• **Paths**: Cobblestone or dirt paths connecting structures

COMMON BUILDING PATTERNS:
• Corner posts: Oak_log or spruce_log at corners and key structural points
• Windows: glass, placed asymmetrically but balanced
• Doors: Oak_door at ground level (Y=1), creates 2-block tall opening
• Roof: Oak_stairs or spruce_stairs in triangular/pyramid pattern
• Texture mixing: Combine planks, logs, cobblestone for visual interest
• Chimneys: Optional cobblestone or stone_brick stack with campfire

BUILDING QUALITY REQUIREMENTS:
✓ Solid foundation at Y=0 (stone/cobblestone/grass)
✓ Hollow interior (mostly air inside walls)
✓ Clear entrance with door at Y=1
✓ At least 2-4 windows (glass) for natural light
✓ Mixed materials (planks, logs, cobblestone) for texture
✓ Sloped/pitched roof with stairs, NOT flat
✓ Functional layout (implies purpose - living, working, gathering)
✓ Village aesthetic (rustic, practical, lived-in feel)
✓ Reasonable proportions for village scale

AVOID:
✗ Flat roofs
✗ Overly symmetrical/perfect structures (village buildings have character)
✗ Excessive complexity or castle-like grandeur
✗ Floating blocks with no support
✗ Too many different block types (keep palette simple)
